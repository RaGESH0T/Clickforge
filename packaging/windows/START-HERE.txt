ClickForge 0.1.0 - Windows package
==================================

QUICK START
-----------
1. Keep ClickForge.exe in this folder (or move it anywhere you like).
2. Double-click ClickForge.exe.
3. Use "Pick on screen" or press F7 to choose a fixed click point.
4. Press F6 to start/stop. Press Esc to stop immediately.

No installer and no administrator rights are required. Windows SmartScreen may
warn that the app is unsigned; choose "More info" then "Run anyway".

See README.md for full instructions, safety limits and troubleshooting.
